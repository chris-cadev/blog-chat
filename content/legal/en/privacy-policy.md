## What this site is

This is a personal blog by Chris Camacho with a public chat attached to each post. There is no account system, no login, and I don't sell personal information.

## What I collect

- **Chat messages** you send (username and text)
- **Username** you choose (stored in a cookie and database)
- **IP address** for basic abuse prevention
- **Language preference** cookie
- **Theme preference** cookie
- **Anonymous analytics** via Umami (no cookies, no cross-site tracking)

## How I use it

- Display chat messages to other visitors
- Keep the chat running and prevent spam/abuse
- Remember your language and theme preferences

## What I don't do

- I don't sell your data
- I don't run ads
- I don't use third-party tracking cookies
- I don't require registration

## Your messages

Messages you send to the public chat are public by design. They may be visible to anyone who visits the site. I may remove messages that violate basic community rules (spam, harassment, illegal content).

## Analytics

This site uses Umami Analytics, a privacy-focused alternative to Google Analytics. Umami does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page. Last updated: September 2026.
