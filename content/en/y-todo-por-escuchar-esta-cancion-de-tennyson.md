**Beautiful World by Tennyson brings back a lot of memories for me.** I found out about it through [this AMV](https://amvnews.ru/files/8391). Back then I liked to feel the music, not in the sense of rhythm or wanting to dance to it, but more digging into the feeling: what the creators wanted to express with the images they shot, the tones they chose, the sounds they included, the lyrics.

Listening to this song and thinking about the relationship I had back then, I put together this set of images in Photoshop. I was trying to translate that feeling into something visual, with a more poetic sense. **Each image is well centered, well cropped, with its meaning.** Those were the tools I had back then.
