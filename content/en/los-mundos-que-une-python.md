Python isn't just a programming language. It's a meeting point between roles that normally don't talk to each other.

## The business world

Managers, product owners, data analysts. Their tools are the spreadsheet, the meeting, the metric. They know what the market wants but don't know what it takes to build it. They tell stories with features, not with code.

## The engineering world

Developers, architects, DevOps. Their tools are the terminal, the IDE, the PR. They know how to build but sometimes forget why it matters. They tell stories with commits, not with impact.

## Where they cross

Python lives in both worlds. A manager can read a pandas notebook without needing a full engineering course. A developer can show a prototype instead of a slide. It's not about one becoming the other — it's about both having a place to meet without stopping being what they are.
