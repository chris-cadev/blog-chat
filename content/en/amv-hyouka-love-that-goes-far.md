Back then I was making AMVs (Anime Music Videos) roughly one every two months. **YouTube was strict about copyright**, so I posted them straight to Facebook. Nothing happened, no alarms went off.

This one is with [Witchcraft by Pendulum](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ogMNV33AhCY), a song I listened to a lot back then, set to Hyouka, an anime I was getting hooked on at that moment.

**I didn't understand English.** In middle school and high school it was barely spoken; it was just to pass the exam. I listened to the song for the tune, the voice, the instruments, the melody, and mostly the rhythm. I didn't know what the lyrics said, and I didn't even have the cultural understanding to know what they referred to. I just listened, liked it, mixed it with an anime, and saw what came out.

I didn't know if what I posted resonated with anyone else. **It was a blind AMV**, made from whatever sounded nice and whatever looked good on screen. Later I started discovering other nuances that had to go into AMVs, and my perfectionism kicked in and decided not to keep going.
