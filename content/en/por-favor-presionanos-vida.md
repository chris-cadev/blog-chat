please, pressure us life
we need to be pressured
when you limit us, juicy creativity comes out
that transforms into the labeled word we use to describe you
ambiguity settles into concreteness, concrete on which we can walk with confidence
ambiguity, from it faith is born, faith that with more pressure transforms into real blood, sweat and tears, product of the transformations we suffer
after watching documentaries on how life has cycled for millennia, I realize more that transforming where we come from, an orbital system, we repeat its pattern orbiting ambiguities
