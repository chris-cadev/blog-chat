I'm living the dream in my actual job hahaha...

Starting in my previous job (2021) I remember dreaming on having a closer interaction with the final user, but at the time I didn't have a clear picture of what that was like...

Now working in manufacturing I really know what it means. Having the person with a rushing voice, not knowing what the problem is and reaching for a fast, secure and not breaking anything patch, now I really know what it is. Knowadays I have that type of requests from my user/cowokers, and I really appreciate to live this time and place when I already some of the quirks of applied tech on running systems.

I think is the part of my learning that most of my team at _dralebrijes_ and _Wrethink_ tried to teach me, to have soft skills. The definition of soft skills I'm more agree on is:

> Abilities transferable across roles and industries.

Some definitions include things like "emotional", "intelligence", etc but I don't agree on those because sometimes the ability to have better problem solving skill on the matter of people are not often tight to an emotional stable/intelligent persona.

I would rather reach that knowledge focused on feeling the people directly first and the I could think on scenarios where the people are being stressed because some button is not doing the whole automation they excepted.
