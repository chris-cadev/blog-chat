A chain of casual clicks led me to a fighting game I'd never heard of. It started with a video that showed up in my YouTube subscriptions: [The real problem with the recent React hack... - Awesome](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQPqn8ltJpk)

I stopped at a [comment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQPqn8ltJpk&lc=UgwiMiS0uJeIlou7rHJ4AaABAg) that made me laugh:

> [@Patterner](https://www.youtube.com/@Patterner): someone misunderestimates my ability to catastrophize.

Curiosity led me to the author's channel. There I found this video of him playing something I couldn't find anywhere online: [Combat License increased to level 24! - Patterner](https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/47FIjPsrBvc?si=T9l1oT1zRcYYtWZF)

That video made me search for the original game name: [Combat License game](https://www.google.com/search?client=firefox-b-d&q=Combat+License+game). The search didn't lead to the game I think, but it led me to this game on Steam: [Your Only Move Is HUSTLE](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2212330/Your_Only_Move_Is_HUSTLE/).

Then on YouTube I found this demo video:

And that's where the chain ended — I added it to my wishlist.

Your Only Move Is HUSTLE is a fighting game with a twist: every move is planned with frame anticipation. It's not a traditional fighter where everything depends on reflexes. Here you think one step ahead.
