**My computer was so slow that Terraria was the only game I could record.** I was recording with [Fraps](https://fraps.com/), but the audio kept coming out desynced; the machine just couldn't do more. [Terraria](https://terraria.org/) was the only game that ran at a small enough resolution for the recording not to turn to mush.

So there I am: 15 years old, Fraps running, audio lagging, playing Terraria because it was the only thing my PC could handle recording.
