**College is when I felt the chest pain the most.** A gnawing pain, like a pressure that wouldn't go away. Maybe the [sufflex I was given when I was about 8 years old](/en/golpe-de-niño) has something to do with it, or maybe it was the anxiety of so much homework, projects, and new concepts at UTT.

They gave me something for muscle pain. It didn't work.

**I've been checked by three different doctors**, each with a different opinion. Some prescribed diclofenac, but I stopped taking it when I went to donate blood and was told I shouldn't be taking it. So there it is, no clear diagnosis.

What I do know is that it's still there. From time to time it shows up, like a reminder that something isn't entirely right. Maybe one day I'll sort it out.
