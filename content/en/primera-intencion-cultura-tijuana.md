**This was one of my first intentions to promote Tijuana's culture.** I re-shared the premiere of Feliz Año Tijuana at the Cineteca CECUT, shared from [Dulce Vázquez](https://www.facebook.com/dulce.vazquezm/posts/pfbid02NmmGRpWGvXU95nZt44kA4ZoEUrZoiPnNwvTS7E6jWGTZDq42F5UVeC2c9rDTPV2vl).

I never projected it onto anything that could take it further. My intentions stayed at sharing, at re-posting. **I never got to the point where my hands did something more.**
