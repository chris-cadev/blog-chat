Eras are not like I saw in movies, they are not sudden but rather slow transitions, even with the rapid changes the reality is that those changes my body persives them as simple perturbance that sometimes missalign my current path but rarely truly switch my mentality.

We are already in a new era, and we should be keeping that mentality using the new tech and embracing it as part of the nature of our current symbiotechnological ecosystem.

I should be using the tech as it is and not fighting in my mind why should or should not use it, if it solve some peoples problem then why should I bother, clearly with some knowledge you could be better, but changing completly the context on where the solution born is crystal clear will not solve the problem but rather create a new one.
