This was the kind of joke I used to post on Facebook back around 2012. **I'm not very proud of them**, but that was the vibe: fake suspense, a dumb punchline, and those famous ellipses to force you to hit "see more."

The ellipses were a classic trick: you'd put `... .. .` right before the punchline so the Facebook algorithm would cut off the post and the reader had to expand it. It worked.
