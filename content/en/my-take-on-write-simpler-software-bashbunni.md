Bashbunni shares a point that resonates: in tech we tend to complicate productivity and solve problems that don't exist. His favorite example is Japan's shipping system — paper-based, no real-time tracking, but so reliable that people send their luggage from the airport to the hotel without worrying. Simple systems with fewer moving parts. Consistency over convenience.

## My take

Sometimes we really go deep enough to create a problem in our head, and we don't take full responsibility for not materializing those problems into software or systems.

## Other angles

The video has a comment from [@bruhtusnull](https://www.youtube.com/@bruhtusnull) that adds another layer: simplicity might not be as straightforward as people think.
