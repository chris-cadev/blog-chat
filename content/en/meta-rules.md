I am an engineer, in the explicit sense of the word. I follow rules, and those rules make me adopt certain behaviors. These behaviors make me think in a certain way, and I replicate the loop.

Now, why change rules so often? There is no better rule to follow; the rules always depend on the context.

Even now that I realized that, I didn't expect a second to [consult with ChatGPT] if what I thought was consistent. He told me a term: _<em>contextual adequacy principle</em>_, whose meaning, I'm sorry, leaves me further away from the answer. Then he mentioned something about _meta-rule_... Okay, meta-x has always been something that has haunted me.

My siblings beat me when I was a kid, and it's not uncommon in childhood, but the fact that they made a promise not to hit me on New Year's Day tells me that I was probably the problem; to exist was the problem.

Well, it's a rare thing, but it's not something that stops me... I think.
